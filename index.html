The Outreach Agent

Four jobs. One Agent.

01 · Find

Spots the businesses in your chosen city or region that are most likely to buy from you.

02 · Enrich

Learns everything about each one: who to contact, their phone, email and address, why they are hot, and how to sell to them.

03 · Email

Writes a personal email to each lead and sends it in your name. No copy-paste blasts, and you approve the tone.

04 · Print & post

On request, a personalised brochure or letter lands on your best leads’ desks as a printed hard copy.

Built for

Firms with large deal sizes and a small number of buyers who really matter. The Agent finds those few, learns what they need, and gets you in front of them.
